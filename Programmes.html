Our Programmes

Learn more about our play-based learning initiatives.

Playgroups

Our community-based playgroups are designed to provide structured, play-based learning for children aged 2-5. Facilitated by trained practitioners, these sessions help children develop the cognitive, social and emotional skills they need for school and life. Our programme is for children, we do not just specialise with children coming from disadvanted households, all kids are welcome. We also provided programmes for children with special needs , we make sure that everyone is accomodated and no child or parent feel left out.

  • Duration: 2-hour sessions, 3 times a week.
  • Focus: Language development, problem-solving, social interaction and early numeracy.
  • Locations: Available in various communities across South Africa.

Toy Libraries

Our Toy Libraries are lending libraries filled with a wide range of high-quality, educational toys and resources. They serve as a resource hub for parents, educators, and community organisations, making play-based learning accessible to all.

  • Membership: Open to parents, caregivers and early learning centres.
  • Resources: Thousands of toys, books, puzzles and games for different developmental stages.
  • Impact: Encourages early literacy, numeracy and fine motor skills.
