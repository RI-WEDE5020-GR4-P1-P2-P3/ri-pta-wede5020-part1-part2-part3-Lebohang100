About Us

Our history, mission, and the team behind the work.

Our History

Founded in 1936, Cotlands has a rich and proud history of serving South Africa's most vulnerable children. For over 80 years, we have been at the forefront of early childhood development, adapting our programmes to meet the evolving needs of communities.

Our Mission

To create access to quality, play-based early learning for young children.

Our Vision

A South Africa where every child is school-ready and has the opportunity to reach their full potential.
